# Cluster Maintenance: SLURM Scheduler Upgrade

On Tuesday, September 15th from 8:00AM CT to 5:00PM CT, the new SSCS cluster will undergo a major upgrade to the Slurm scheduler.

<!-- more -->

This upgrade to our job scheduling system will affect all running and queued jobs.

Monday at 5:00PM:

Job submissions will close at 5:00PM CT on September 14th.

Scheduled jobs will be allowed to run until the morning of the 15th on Cronus, Athens and Acropolis. Any jobs that are still running the morning of September 15th will be terminated as part of the Slurm upgrade process.

Before the upgrade:

Please make sure any important work or data is backed up. Plan jobs accordingly and do not submit jobs that are expected to run into the upgrade window.

During the upgrade, users will be unable to submit or run jobs.

If you experience any issues accessing the cluster or submitting jobs once systems are back online, please reach out immediately to [ssc-server-support@lists.uchicago.edu](mailto:ssc-server-support@lists.uchicago.edu) for support.

Thank you,
