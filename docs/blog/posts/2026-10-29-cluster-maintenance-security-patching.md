# Cluster Maintenance: Security Patching

On Monday, November 2nd from 9:00AM CT to 5:00PM CT, the new SSCS cluster will receive important security updates and reboots of the remaining compute and GPU nodes.

<!-- more -->

The maintenance window is expected to conclude by 5:00 PM CT, though we anticipate finishing sooner.

This maintenance will affect Athens, Cronus, and new Acropolis users.

During this time, nodes will be drained and rebooted, so running jobs may be
interrupted. Login node sessions, including VNC, will also end. In
preparation, please take the following steps before the maintenance window
begins:

-  Ensure all running jobs and workflows are completed prior to the window
-  Checkpoint long-running jobs where possible to prevent loss of progress
-  Avoid submitting new jobs that could extend into the maintenance window
-  Save your work and log out of login node sessions, including VNC

We will send a follow-up notification once maintenance is complete.

If you have any questions or need assistance planning around the maintenance
window, please contact us at ssc-server-support@lists.uchicago.edu.

Thank you,

Social Sciences Computing Services Team
