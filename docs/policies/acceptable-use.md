# Usage Policy

The SSCS cluster is intended for academic research and instructional use by authorized University of Chicago faculty, students, and staff. Access is limited to members of the Social Sciences Division and their affiliates.

Users are expected to use cluster resources responsibly and considerately. This includes avoiding unnecessary or prolonged monopolization of shared compute resources and ensuring that workloads are appropriate for the academic and research mission of the division.

The following uses are not permitted:

- Unauthorized access or use of cluster resources
- Commercial workloads unrelated to the University's academic or research mission
- Activities that intentionally interfere with the availability or performance of cluster resources for other users

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of the cluster complies with applicable University policies and requirements.

For questions about permitted use or to verify eligibility, contact the [Cluster Support team](./../frequently-asked-questions.md#contact-support).
